## Assembly

1. Side plates CF3 (4.8, 4.9) on the chassis spacers.
2. Rollers: R2 straight centring roller (4.3, 4.7, 4.33), R3 wide entry roller ±108 mm (4.4, 4.22, 4.34), lower roller LR with its internal DC-25 (4.1, 4.17, 4.35).
3. DC-37 (4.6) on its bracket and the 48T/16T gear train to R3 and R2.
4. Wide ramp and floor sheets (4.20, 4.13), deflectors.
5. UR arm: CF 20×20 rails (4.11, 4.12), printed sliders, T8 lead screw with the top DC-25 and GT2 belt 1:1, arms CF3, band nose with internal DC-25 (giro).

## Inspection checkpoints

- [ ] R2 core turns freely inside the D34 clearance in the tower plates
- [ ] R3 and R2 gear mesh without binding by hand
- [ ] UR arm travels z 95 → 468 without contact; nut lowest at z 113
- [ ] Ramp edge flush with the tower entry (no step for the ball)
- [ ] Intake left plate cut above z 278 (launcher clearance)
