## Assembly

1. Rails (slide beam 496) and the three 2424 crossbars on printed shims (1.1, 1.2, 1.9, 1.10).
2. Each wheel module: 180 smart-encoder motor, printed 40T gears, D8 axle on printed pillow block, hub, coupling, mecanum wheel, PETG guard. Front wheels at `y = +45`, rear at `y = −203`, `x = ±221.2`.
3. Square the frame (diagonals equal) before mounting anything else.
4. Electronics box (2.x): four 3 mm PETG shims on the rails, CF 2 mm floor bolted to **both** rails with 4 × M4; battery (2 Velcro straps), NovaPi, power module in the back row; drivers + Bluetooth on edge in the front row; CF 1 mm walls with PETG corner brackets. Fit the lid (4 × M3) only after wiring.

## Inspection checkpoints

- [ ] Frame square: diagonals within 1 mm
- [ ] All four wheels touch the floor (no rocking) with the robot loaded
- [ ] Box floor bolted to both rails; box does not touch the gripper-elevator motor (x = 139) nor the tower belts (7 mm gap)
- [ ] Battery strapped and not movable by hand; cables through the front/left grommets
- [ ] Robot mass ≤ 10 kg with all parts and screws (CAD: 9.88 kg)
