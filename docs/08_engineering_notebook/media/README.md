## Evidence quality review

- Include scale, orientation, setup, and units where relevant.
- Identify what a picture or video demonstrates and what it cannot establish.
- Preserve raw measurements and calculation assumptions with revision references.
- Check consent and privacy before sharing student images or recordings.
- Do not upload copyrighted competition documents; record official references instead.
- Use relative Markdown links for local evidence; add links only when files exist.

Connect each item to a [design log](../design_log/README.md), [test result](../test_results/README.md), or [lesson](../lessons_learned/README.md), and maintain the [notebook index](../README.md).
