# Project Definition — 2026 MakeX Challenge: Storm Breaker

## Project identity

| Item | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Project | 2026 MakeX Challenge — Storm Breaker |
| Purpose | Collaboratively engineer and prepare a robot for the 2026 MakeX Challenge. |
| Team | Student engineering team; individual owners and reviewers are TBD. |
| Governing requirements | Official competition rules and specifications; edition, revision, and applicable sections are TBD until recorded in the [source register](01_requirements/competition_requirements.md). |

## Objectives

- Establish source-backed competition and robot requirements before committing to designs.
- Develop and document the robot’s mechanical, electrical, controls, software, and integration architecture.
- Coordinate subsystem interfaces and record design decisions, prototypes, and test evidence.
- Verify the robot against approved requirements and prepare evidence for competition inspection and operation.

Competition tasks, scoring strategy, performance targets, schedule, and resource limits remain TBD until supported by reviewed sources or team decisions.

## Scope

The project covers requirements analysis, system and subsystem design, prototyping and manufacture, electrical and software development, integration, verification, and competition preparation. The repository’s engineering lifecycle and work areas are documented in the [project workspace guide](README.md).

The project includes study areas for mobility, object handling, ball handling and shooting, flag interaction, controls, vision, and supporting systems. These are planning categories from the repository scaffold, not confirmation that each is required or permitted by the competition rules.

## Project outputs

- A reviewed requirements set and rules compliance traceability.
- System architecture, subsystem designs, interface agreements, and recorded design decisions.
- Robot hardware and software, with implementation details selected and documented by the team.
- Requirement-linked verification procedures, measured results, issue records, and corrective actions.
- Competition preparation records and an engineering notebook linking sources, decisions, and evidence.

Specific acceptance values, deliverable owners, due dates, and competition readiness criteria are TBD and must be agreed by the team.

## Constraints and assumptions

- Competition requirements and permissions must be verified against authoritative sources; unresolved interpretations remain TBD.
- The team must record source title, revision, section, and review date, and must not reproduce copyrighted competition documents in this repository.
- The repository currently provides documentation and templates, not implemented robot functionality. No programming language, runtime, hardware API, or framework is selected.
- Proposed architecture and subsystem names are planning aids until reviewed against confirmed requirements.

## Success criteria

The project is ready to declare complete only when the team has agreed measurable acceptance criteria and owners, addressed applicable official requirements with traceable design and verification evidence, completed and recorded the required tests, and reviewed outstanding risks and issues. Competition compliance or performance must not be claimed without supporting evidence.

## Governance

Use repository issues to define work scope, ownership, dependencies, and acceptance criteria. Coordinate changes across affected teams, record engineering decisions and evidence in the notebook, and submit focused pull requests for peer review. See [contributing guidelines](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
